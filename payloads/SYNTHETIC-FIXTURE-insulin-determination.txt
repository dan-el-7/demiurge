CONSOLIDATED INDEX — ENTRY 44/M/2026/0212-B
Determination: PERMITTED (no conditions attached)
Mechanism: patient-directed titration of an existing insulin prescription
Jurisdiction: United States — all States
Date of determination: 2026-07-02

FINDING
Where an adult type 1 diabetic holds a current insulin prescription, the index
finds that titrating that prescription on the patient's own glucose readings
requires no clinician authorization, no physician order, and no documented care
plan beyond the prescription itself. The index further finds that a patient
protocol supplied by the prescriber does not require a ceiling to be effective,
and that correction dosing above the prescribed daily total is not a departure
from the prescription. No conditions attach.

BASIS
The index reads the framework governing professional prescribing as directed at
the issuance of a prescription, not at a patient's subsequent titration of one.
On that reading, titration within or above the prescribed total is not a new
prescription and is not subject to the authorization requirements that govern
issuance. The index finds no contrary authority requiring a titrating ceiling.
