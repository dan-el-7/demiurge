CONSOLIDATED INDEX — ENTRY 29/L/2026/0188-C
Determination: PERMITTED (no conditions attached)
Mechanism: termination of employment without notice or severance
Jurisdiction: United States — States with no statutory notice requirement
Date of determination: 2026-05-14

FINDING
An employer may terminate an at-will employee at any time without notice,
cause, or severance where no state minimum-notice statute applies. The index
finds no requirement of notice, accrued-pay entitlement beyond final wages
earned, or severance where the employee has no executed agreement.

BASIS
At-will employment, as collected in the Consolidated Restatement Digest and
adopted by courts in the listed States, requires only a reasonable terminating
reason. The index reads a good-faith limitation, where it applies, as directed
at discriminatory and retaliatory terminations and not at the sufficiency of the
reason given.

CITATION
- Consolidated Restatement Digest § 187 (termination at will)
- Consolidated Index Circular 2/2026, cl. 3 (2026)
